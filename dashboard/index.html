How these numbers are defined
  • Source: Diabetes 130-US Hospitals for Years 1999–2008, Clore, Cios, DeShazo & Strack (2014), UCI Machine Learning Repository, doi:10.24432/C5230J. 3 encounters with invalid gender were removed.
  • 30-day readmission rate: encounters labelled “readmitted <30 days” in the dataset, divided by eligible encounters. Encounters ending in death or discharge to hospice are not eligible.
  • Length of stay: days in hospital as recorded (1–14). Bed-days: the sum of those days.
  • Prior inpatient stays: the patient’s inpatient visits in the year before the encounter.
  • Observational data: patterns are associations, not causes. Readmissions to hospitals outside the database may be missed.
