How these numbers are defined
  • Length of stay: discharge date minus admission date, in days (same-day stays count as 0).
  • 30-day readmission rate: share of stays followed by another admission of the same patient within 30 days of discharge. Stays ending in death and discharges in the final 30 days of the data are left out, because their follow-up is incomplete.
  • Cost: total billed cost of the stay; the 1.5% of stays with no recorded cost are left out of cost figures.
  • Data: synthetic and fully de-identified, generated to demonstrate the analysis method. Patterns are associations, not medical evidence.
